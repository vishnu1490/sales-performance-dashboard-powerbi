# 📊 Sales Performance Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Overview

This project is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze and visualize sales performance across multiple dimensions including **products, customers, time, and geography**. It enables users to compare actual sales against budget, identify trends, and uncover key business insights.

---

## 🎯 Key Objectives

* Monitor overall sales performance vs budget
* Identify top-performing products and customers
* Analyze monthly sales trends
* Understand customer distribution across cities
* Enable data-driven decision making

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ Sales Overview

* KPI card highlighting **Sales vs Budget performance**
* Monthly sales trend analysis
* Product category contribution (donut chart)
* Top 10 customers and products
* Year and month slicers for dynamic filtering

---

### 2️⃣ Customer Details

* Sales distribution by **customer city**
* Top 10 customers by revenue
* Customer-level monthly performance (heatmap table)
* Gender-based sales analysis
* Interactive filters for category, sub-category, and product

---

### 3️⃣ Product Details

* Sales breakdown by **product sub-category**
* Top 10 products by sales
* Monthly sales vs budget comparison
* Detailed product-level performance table
* Drill-down capability for deeper insights

---

## 🧾 Data Model

The dashboard is built using a structured data model:

* `FACT_InternetSales` – sales transactions
* `FACT_Budget` – budget data
* `DIM_Customers` – customer information
* `DIM_Products` – product hierarchy
* `DIM_Calendar` – date and time analysis

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Power BI Desktop
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* Power Query (ETL & data transformation)

---

## ✨ Features

* Interactive slicers (Year, Month, City, Category)
* Dynamic visuals and drill-down capabilities
* KPI indicators with performance comparison
* Clean and user-friendly dashboard layout

---

## ▶️ How to Use

1. Download the `.pbix` file
2. Open it in Power BI Desktop
3. Use slicers to filter data by year, month, or category
4. Navigate between pages for detailed insights

---

## 🚀 Insights Delivered

* Sales consistently track against budget with visible seasonal trends
* Certain product categories (e.g., Bikes) dominate revenue contribution
* Top customers significantly impact total sales
* Geographic analysis highlights key revenue-generating cities
